Put your disc images here, with any file names. build.py finds each one by its content.

- PlayStation, USA (SLUS-00067): the .bin files (and the .cue). Required.
- PSP, Castlevania: The Dracula X Chronicles, Europe (ULES-00841): the .iso or .cso. Optional: Maria.
- Sega Saturn, Japan (T-9527G): the .bin files (and the .cue). Optional: the Saturn stages.
- ps1vram_uk.bin of the Ukrainian Xbox 360 mod. Optional: 10 Ukrainian labels (file select, HUD).

Nothing in this folder goes into git.
