Tap a room to teleport. Drag to pan, pinch or wheel to zoom.

1. Click a tile in the palette (Shift or Ctrl+click, or drag: several tiles). 2. Click the room to place it; drag to paint. Right-drag or Shift+drag on the room: select cells, their tiles light up in the palette. Alt+click: take the tile under the mouse. Middle or Space+drag: pan. Wheel: zoom. Ctrl+C: selection to brush. H / V: flip the selection (or the brush) left-right / upside down, the tile art too, Del: restore the game tiles, Esc: drop the selection, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo / redo.
